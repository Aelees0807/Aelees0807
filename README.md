## ABOUT.ME / CURRENT.MISSION

Third-year Computer Engineering student at CHARUSAT, spending most of my time moving between full-stack builds and data/ML work. I like taking a rough idea — an academic workflow, an expense tracker, a diagnostic tool — and getting it to a state where someone can actually open it and use it, not just read about it in a report.

Right now that means building an image-classification platform in PyTorch, picking away at a DSA sheet in the evenings, and getting steadily more comfortable on the data-science side — pandas, scikit-learn, and enough statistics to know when a model's results actually mean something.
